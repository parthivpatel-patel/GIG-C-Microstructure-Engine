## Known visualization limits

* Quote positions use binary64 approximations;server exact rational values remain the
  financial authority. No L1-to-depth inference or theoretical-to-observed relabeling.
* Comparison baselines may have different source times;shared bps does not imply a
  synchronized investment return. Global256-row retention can leave quiet symbols with
  too few quotes. Missing variation remains explicitly unavailable.
* Trade plots are unreconciled prints,not a complete corrected tape. GARCH is a declared
  stationary toy profile,not calibrated model selection or predictive evidence.
* Fullscreen is browser-capability dependent with visible failure fallback. Long-run
  physical GPU memory,FPS,screen-reader and real touch-camera behavior remain untested.
* Optional Three/OrbitControls chunk remains above500kB and emits its existing warning;
  it is deferred,not removed or hidden by a raised warning limit.
