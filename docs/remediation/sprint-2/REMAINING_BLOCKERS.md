the estimator, population, side aggregation, normalization or acceptance oracle.

   concentration. Approval would establish a prospective contract; it would not
   prove the unknown historical requirement had always specified HHI.

   units, normalization, missingness and independent oracle approved first; implement
   separately only if justified. Do not silently swap formulas to close a finding.
