## Private release decision and claim limits

Record the owner acceptance decision pergate and workflow, with exact source identity.
Bounded SEC/offline research may be accepted whilelive data remains blocked. A full
operational private release cannot be accepted if its essential live/universe workflows
remain unqualified. Even a private accepted release is not institutional production,
real-market full L3 validation, a calibrated forecasting service or a trading system.
